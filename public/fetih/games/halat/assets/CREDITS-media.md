# Halat Çekme: media credits and licences

Everything in `games/halat/assets/{audio,sprites,textures,fonts}` (built by `tools/halat/build-media.mjs`) is either
CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), SIL OFL 1.1 (fonts), or original work made procedurally by the build script
(sprites, textures, synthesised sounds; released as CC0 like the rest of the project's own assets).
No attribution is legally required for CC0, but we credit the authors anyway.

Rebuild: `node tools/halat/build-media.mjs` (needs ffmpeg/ffprobe on PATH and `tools/node_modules`).

## Synthesised / procedural (made by build-media.mjs, no third-party source)

- Audio: `countdown_beep`, `countdown_go`, `join_pop` (pure synthesis); `rope_snap` (synthesised whoosh/crack/chirp + Kenney plank impact);
  `drum_roll`, `drum_loop` (sequenced from the drum_hit/drum_tek samples credited above); `tractor_start` (variable-speed playback of the tractor recording).
- Sprites: all of `sprites/` (fx_dust, fx_dust_sheet, fx_smoke, fx_sweat, fx_strain_lines, fx_splash_sheet, fx_mud_blob, fx_confetti, fx_sparkle, fx_star, fx_ring, fx_glow, fx_heart, fx_impact_star) drawn as SVG and rasterised with resvg.
- Textures: all of `textures/` (tex_grass, tex_grass_normal, tex_dirt, tex_mud, tex_mud_normal, tex_water_normal, tex_rope, tex_rope_normal, tex_wood, tex_canvas_stripes, tex_sky_gradient) generated from tileable value noise.

## Modifications

Audio was cut (segments chosen by RMS-envelope / silence detection), layered, pitch/speed-shifted, filtered,
loop-crossfaded, loudness-normalised (K-weighted gated loudness, peak-limited to about -2 dBFS, -4 dBFS for a few very transient sounds) and converted to
Ogg Vorbis 44.1 kHz (mono for sound effects, stereo for music).
